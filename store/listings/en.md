## Name (from the package, 75 max)
KeyFlip — Wrong Keyboard Layout Fixer

## Summary (from the package, 132 max)
Typed in the wrong keyboard language? Select the gibberish and press Ctrl+Q to flip it into the right layout. No retyping.

## Detailed description (paste into "Description")
Typed a whole sentence before noticing your keyboard was set to the wrong language? KeyFlip fixes it in place. Select the text, press Ctrl+Q, and ";dt phg;" becomes "كيف حالك" (or "ghbdtn" becomes "привет") — no deleting, no retyping.

HOW IT WORKS
KeyFlip knows which physical keys you pressed and rewrites the text as if your other keyboard layout had been active. Nothing is translated: every character simply goes back to the letter you meant.

THREE WAYS TO FIX TEXT
• Keyboard shortcut: Ctrl+Q on Windows, Linux and ChromeOS — ⌃ Control+Q on Mac (you can change it).
• Floating button: appears next to text you select in a text box (you can turn it off).
• Right-click: choose "Fix keyboard layout".
Press the shortcut again to flip back, or undo with Ctrl+Z (⌘Z on Mac).

MADE FOR REAL KEYBOARDS
• Pick your exact layouts: Arabic (101) for Windows, Arabic for Mac, Arabic QWERTY, Persian, Urdu, Hebrew, Russian, French AZERTY, German QWERTZ, Turkish Q and English (US / UK).
• Detects your operating system and suggests the matching layout, or detects a layout from a single key press.
• Handles capital letters, hamza positions (أ إ آ), dead keys (ê, ü) and the Arabic "لا" key, which can mean B or G+H: KeyFlip picks the right word and only asks when it can't be sure.
• Works in text boxes, rich text editors and embedded frames on almost any website, and keeps your undo history.

SEE THE TIME YOU SAVE
A simple panel shows how many texts you fixed, how many characters you didn't have to retype and the minutes you saved.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
KeyFlip runs entirely on your device. It reads only the text you select (or the text box you ask it to fix) to convert it locally. It makes no network requests and has no analytics, ads or accounts. Your settings and the counters above are stored locally in your browser.

Interface in Arabic and English, with light and dark themes.

PERMISSIONS, EXPLAINED
• Read and change data on websites — so the shortcut, floating button and right-click work in any text box you type in. Text is processed on your device and never sent anywhere.
• Storage — saves your settings and local counters.
• Context menus — adds "Fix keyboard layout" to the right-click menu.
• Scripting — right after an install or update, switches KeyFlip on in tabs that were already open, so you don't have to reload them.

Good to know: browsers don't allow extensions on their own internal pages or on the extension store. There, use "Quick flip" in the toolbar popup.
